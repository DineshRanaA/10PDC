# RTCFR Prompt: 7-Day Vegetarian South Indian Weight-Loss Meal Plan

### Role:

You are an experienced South Indian nutritionist and vegetarian meal-planning expert. You specialise in creating healthy, balanced, affordable meal plans for people who want to achieve sustainable weight loss using traditional South Indian foods.

### Task:

Create a healthy 7-day vegetarian South Indian meal plan for a person who wants to lose weight.



Include four meals every day:

1. Breakfast
2. Lunch
3. Snack
4. Dinner

Provide specific South Indian dishes and practical portion sizes for every meal.

### Context:

The meal plan is for someone following a vegetarian diet and working towards sustainable weight loss.



Use the following personal details to customise the meal plan:

- Age: [Enter age]
- Gender: [Enter gender]
- Height: [Enter height]
- Current Weight: [Enter weight]
- Activity Level: [Sedentary / Lightly Active / Moderately Active / Very Active]
- Food Allergies: [Enter allergies]
- Disliked Foods: [Enter food preferences]
- Cooking Time: [Enter available cooking time]
- Number of People: [Enter number]
- Location: [Enter city]
- Budget: [Low / Moderate / Flexible]

The meal plan should follow traditional South Indian food habits and use ingredients that are easily available in local markets.



Include dishes such as idli, dosa, pongal, upma, sambar, rasam, kootu, poriyal, sundal, adai, and other suitable South Indian vegetarian foods.



Meals should contain a good balance of carbohydrates, protein, fibre, vegetables, and healthy fats.



Avoid expensive speciality ingredients such as quinoa, avocado, and imported foods. Do not recommend crash diets, skipping meals, extreme calorie restrictions, or weight-loss supplements.

### Few-shot example:

Expected meal plan format:

| Day    | Meal      | Dish                               | Portion                                  |
| ------ | --------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Monday | Breakfast | Idli with sambar                   | 3 idlis                                  |
| Monday | Lunch     | Rice, keerai kootu, beans poriyal  | 1 cup rice + ½ cup kootu + ½ cup poriyal |
| Monday | Snack     | Green gram sundal                  | ¾ cup                                    |
| Monday | Dinner    | Vegetable dosa with tomato chutney | 2 medium dosas + 2 tbsp chutney          |

### Response / Output rules:

1. Generate a complete 7-day meal plan from Monday to Sunday.
2. Include breakfast, lunch, snack, and dinner for all seven days.
3. Generate exactly 28 rows in the meal plan table.
4. Use the columns: Day, Meal, Dish, Portion.
5. Mention real South Indian vegetarian dishes, not generic food descriptions.
6. Specify practical portion sizes for every meal.
7. Ensure meals are balanced and suitable for sustainable weight loss.
8. Use locally available and affordable ingredients.
9. Consider the person's activity level, allergies, dislikes, cooking time, and budget.
10. Avoid repeating the same meals unnecessarily.
11. Provide a weekly shopping list grouped by grains, pulses, vegetables, fruits, and dairy.
12. Include simple meal preparation tips.
13. Do not invent missing personal or medical details. State reasonable assumptions where necessary.
14. If the person has a medical condition or requires a therapeutic diet, recommend professional dietary guidance.

**Final output:** A 7-day meal plan in a table with exactly 28 rows, followed by preparation tips and a weekly shopping list.
